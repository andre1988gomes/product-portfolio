## Core Principle

The riskiest assumptions are often the ones that feel obvious and remain untested.

## Step 1 - Identify Assumptions

Capture assumptions across:

- user and behavior
- value and outcomes
- feasibility and constraints
- ecosystem and stakeholders

## Step 2 - Classify Assumptions

Assess each assumption by:

- certainty
- impact if wrong

Treat high-impact, low-certainty assumptions as critical.

## Step 3 - Select The Highest-Risk Assumption

Choose one assumption that would invalidate the initiative if it failed.

## Step 4 - Define Disconfirming Signals

State what evidence would support the assumption and what evidence would invalidate it.

## Step 5 - Design Discovery Activities

Choose the minimum activities required to reduce uncertainty.

## Step 6 - Decide

Proceed, pivot, or stop, then document why.
