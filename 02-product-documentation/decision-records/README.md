# Decision Records

This folder contains **product decision records**.

These documents capture **why** a decision was made, not just **what** was implemented.

Their purpose is to:
- preserve decision context over time
- make trade-offs explicit
- avoid re-litigating past decisions without new information
- support accountability and learning

Decision records are written when a decision is **non-trivial**, **irreversible**, or **strategically significant**.

---

## What These Records Are

Each decision record documents:
- the context in which the decision was made
- the options that were seriously considered
- the risks and trade-offs involved
- the final decision and its rationale

They are intentionally concise and factual.

---

## What These Records Are Not

Decision records are **not**:
- post-hoc justifications
- technical design documents
- meeting minutes
- consensus statements

Disagreement is expected and documented when relevant.

---

## When I Use Decision Records

I create a decision record when:
- multiple viable options exist
- the cost of reversing the decision is high
- ownership boundaries are affected
- the decision materially impacts outcomes or risk

If a decision can be safely changed later, it does not need a record.
