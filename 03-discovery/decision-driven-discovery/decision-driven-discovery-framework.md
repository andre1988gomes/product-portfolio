# Decision-Driven Discovery Framework

## Evidence Metadata

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Used in | [Discovery Pivot](../../01-case-studies/case-study-discovery-pivot/case-study.md) and reusable across discovery work |
| Decision supported | Stop discovery when the decision risk is lower than the delivery risk. |
| Evidence type | Framework |
| Confidence level | High |
| Outcome influenced | Better decision timing and less unnecessary discovery work. |

## Core Principle

Discovery is complete when the decision risk is lower than the delivery risk.

## Step 1 - Define The Decision

State the concrete decision discovery must enable.

## Step 2 - Identify Decision-Critical Unknowns

Focus on the unknowns that would materially change the decision.

## Step 3 - Set The Exit Criterion

Define what evidence will be sufficient to decide before discovery starts.

## Step 4 - Run Targeted Discovery

Execute only the activities that reduce decision risk.

## Step 5 - Evaluate Signal Convergence

Ask whether the signals are consistent enough and whether more discovery would actually change direction.

## Step 6 - Decide And Commit

Proceed, pivot, or stop. Then document the rationale and accepted risks.
